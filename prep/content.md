# Content
Topic: **Financial Math**

## Overview/Introduction/Context

Financial math is using math to solve money-related problems. To elaborate, it helps in calculating interest, understanding loans, and overall deciding between financial options. Some people who use financial math are banks, investors, businesses, and companies. It also helps let us know how much our savings will grow, how much interest you will pay on a loan, and what our future money will be worth. It teaches us the basics of how an individual can grow and learn to make smarter financial decisions In addition, the upcoming modern date of technology could be the next big step in supporting the financial math field for it could be an improvement.
